# DEAD CURRENT — Phase 4: RPG Layer Plan

Status: accepted 2026-09-29 after human playtest of the Engineering, Survival, and Persuasion builds. This document is the plan as it was executed.

## Mission

Prove that the character the player builds changes how they experience the same world.

The loop is:

**Allocate a small build → walk into content that already exists → a check opens an option someone else does not get → the build is still there after save/load**

This phase is not a character system. It adds the smallest Attributes → Skills → Perks chain, wires it into the shared rule language, and spends it on Shore Watch, the Wrecked Survey Launch, and the Sounder Chart. No new place, no new quest, no new enemy.

The target feeling is:

"I made a person, and that person can see or say something here that another person cannot."

# 2. Player-Facing Target

One prototype allocation screen and the existing canvas journal.

The player spends a small pool:

- 3 attribute points, each attribute 0–2
- 2 skill points, each skill 0–2
- 1 perk, chosen from 3

Three orientations are enough to test:

- Grasp + Engineering
- Fieldcraft + Survival
- Bearing + Persuasion

Each orientation opens different information or a different line in content that is already on `Lvl_Boathouse`. Tab shows the build. F5 / F9 restores it. An older save loads as an unspent build, so the player can still allocate.

# 4. Model (PROVISIONAL)

Not Fallout SPECIAL. Each attribute is the capacity behind one skill, so the chain is obvious and a later skill is another row, not a rewrite.

| Attribute | What it is | Linked skill |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grasp | reading made things: panels, housings, wiring | Engineering |
| Fieldcraft | reading ground, animals, and how people moved | Survival |
| Bearing | holding a conversation and pressing for what was withheld | Persuasion |

**Effective skill** = invested skill ranks + 1 if the linked attribute is 2 or higher, otherwise + 0.

Checks that say `SkillAtLeast` compare the effective skill. Checks that say `AttributeAtLeast` compare the raw attribute. A perk is owned or not; perks are not ranks.

Why this shape: a skill point and an attribute point are both ways to reach a check, and neither is a percent. With only 2 skill points, one skill can be raised to 2. Raising its attribute to 2 adds a further +1, which is how attributes matter without a second math system. A character who spreads every attribute to 1 gets no bonus. Specializing is the point.

Failed checks are **hidden**. A choice or inspect variant whose conditions fail does not appear. There is no greyed-out row in this phase. When a visible choice or the active inspect variant required an attribute, skill, or perk, the prompt shows that requirement in DEAD CURRENT's own form: `[Engineering 2]`, `[Fieldcraft 2]`, `[Schematic Eye]`. The label is generated from the condition. Authors do not type it into the line.

New story text from these checks is PROVISIONAL.

# 5. Architecture

- `UDCCharacterProgressionComponent` on `ADCPlayerCharacter` owns ranks and perks. The character class does not grow a field per skill.
- Lookup is by Gameplay Tag (`Attribute.Grasp`, `Skill.Engineering`, `Perk.SchematicEye`). The condition's `Id` is that tag's name. `Quantity` is the minimum for attribute and skill checks.
- `FDCRuleContext` carries the component. `UDCGameplayRules` evaluates the new conditions. Dialogue, quests, and inspectables do not grow a second API.
- Validation rejects an attribute, skill, or perk id that is not in the catalog.
- The catalog is a small C++ table (tag, display name, link, perk text). Adding a later entry is a row plus a tag. Consumers keep using the condition.
- The build panel is canvas HUD, same as inventory. Key **B** opens it. **F1–F3** raise attributes, **F4–F6** skills, **F7–F9** take a perk, **F10** resets the prototype allocation. Digit keys stay with dialogue.
- Save version 5 stores attribute ranks, skill ranks, and owned perks. Empty means the default unspent build, which is what a save from before this phase receives.

No actor branches on `if Engineering >= 2 && TalkingToMara`. Content authors a condition.

## RP-04 — Engineering, Survival, Persuasion, attribute

All of these are extra variants or one extra Mara reply. The previous text remains the fallback.

- **Mara**, after her storm deflection (`Skill.Persuasion` effective ≥ 2): she can be pressed once. She admits she saw two people come up off that beach and would not follow. She does not explain the Current, and her faction stays undecided. Sets `wreck.mara_pressed`.

## RP-05 — Perk proof and the Sounder Chart

One perk point, three perks, each consumed by an inspect variant:

- **Schematic Eye** (`Perk.SchematicEye`): with the Sounder Chart in inventory, the depth sounder yields the margin note (the last tick marked not a shoal). This is the chart hook.
- **Engineering** effective ≥ 2, also with the chart, and without the perk: a lesser reading, that the trace and the chart are the same spacing. The perk variant is listed first and includes that fact so it is not lost.
- **Pulse Read** (`Perk.PulseRead`): the dead fish took one shock together, not a tide.
- **Relay Ear** (`Perk.RelayEar`): after the relay has already been inspected (the Shore Watch clue still happens for everyone), the housing was seated by someone who knew the pinout.

**Done when:** at least the chart perk is reachable in play, and the other two perks are real variants rather than unused tags.

# 9. Acceptance Scenario (manual, for Anthony)

1. Launch `Lvl_Boathouse`. Press **B**. Spend Grasp 2 and Engineering 2. Take Schematic Eye.
2. Tab shows that build.
3. At the wreck, the breaker panel offers an Engineering reading the unspent character does not get. Pulling the leads still works.
4. Loot the Sounder Chart from the tender. The depth sounder now says more, including the Schematic Eye margin.
5. Reset with **F10** (prototype only). Spend Fieldcraft 2 and Survival 2. Take Pulse Read.
6. The life jackets and the chalk warning say more. The dead fish say more. The breaker does not.
7. Reset again. Spend Bearing 2 and Persuasion 2.
8. Read the survey log, talk to Mara, ask about the storm, and press her. She gives one limited admission and will not repeat it.
9. F5, quit, relaunch, F9. The build is unchanged. Shore Watch and the wreck still play as before.
