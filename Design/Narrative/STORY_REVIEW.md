## Strongest New Ideas

1. **Islanding.** The collapse became permanent because the network was deliberately cut into self-sufficient sections, "islanded" in grid terms, on the collapse night (the Severance). The archipelago's isolation is partly engineered. Connection vs isolation becomes a literal electrical question as well as a political one.
2. **Stale priority tables.** If the network rejoins, it runs the region by sixty-year-old priorities: it powers a flooded hospital and cuts a living settlement's heat. The catastrophe is regional and material, not world-ending.
3. **Liv holding the Line.** The missing sister is in the offshore crib refusing the network's rejoin requests by hand, night after night. Rescuing her means letting go of the line. The rescue conflicts with the stakes.
4. **The Four-Mile Crib.** The endgame is an offshore water-intake crib reached by boat or through a four-mile intake tunnel under the lake bed. It could not exist in any other setting.
5. **Lighthouse characteristics.** The Pointe Sombre light began flashing the pattern instead of its own charted flash signature, so ships misread it and wrecked. The vertical slice's mystery is built on real navigation practice.
6. **Existing content reread, not rewritten.** "HEAR IT TOO" is Liv's hand; the Relay Ear "knew the pinout" reading is Liv's coil; "Current events" was ordinary public hazard jargon before it was a myth. No asset changes needed.
