## Findings

### G-01 — important

- **Where:** `Tools/EditorScripts/create_dialogue.py`, node `reward_coil` (the line that pays out `done_coil`). State: coil route, the moment the flag `shore.relay_recovered` is set, before `ADCConditionalPresence` moves Mara.
- **Behavior:** Handing over the coil makes Mara say: "I'm going to sit up with this coil tonight and listen." Deferral keeps her at the lookout for that line, which is correct. The player then walks to the landing and finds her on the deck, pack beside a lashed crate, skiff loaded. The line they just heard promised she would stay up with the coil. The place shows the watch getting ready to leave.
- **Why it matters:** This is the coil route's only spoken setup for the new picture. The walkthrough asks whether the stage feels like the watch getting ready to travel. The line answers the other way, a few seconds earlier. `done_coil` ("The coil talked all night. I'm writing down what I can. He's still walking the shore.") can still be said on the deck; it does not promise she is staying. `reward_coil` does.
- **Reproduction:** New game. Take the coil, turn it in at the lookout, read the reward line, walk to the landing.
- **Smallest correction:** Change that one clause in `reward_coil` so it does not say she will sit up and listen tonight. Do not name a destination or explain the Current. Record it as the one dialogue spec change the phase plan allows. Leave `done_coil`, `went_back`, and the kill-route lines alone.

### G-02 — important (question for Anthony, not a silent layout change)

- **Where:** Stage placement in `Tools/EditorScripts/build_landing_stage.py` (`MARA_ON_STAGE` (960, −990), deck and gangway south of the curb) against `ADCScavengerController` sight (radius 1800 cm, 75° peripheral) and the south patrol leg in `build_boathouse.py` (Y −350, X 2000..2700, facing along that leg). Spec line: "Possible danger: none" / "No fighting at the stage."
- **Behavior:** The scavenger still only chases the player, and his patrol points are unchanged. On the coil route he is alive. From the southwest corner of the patrol (2000, −350), facing west, the deck and the gangway sit about 11 m away and roughly 20–35° south of his forward vector, inside both the radius and the cone. The beach between the path and the stage is flat (floor at Z 0, curb 18 cm). Nothing blocks that line. Standing on the stage to read the coil picture can start a chase.
- **Why it matters:** The coil payoff is "walk back and look." That standing spot is inside an existing hostile's sight. The reward line already says he is still out there, so some danger is intended. The spec's "no danger" is still wrong, and a chase will pull Anthony off the stage before he can judge it. The kill route does not have this problem (the scavenger is dead).
- **Reproduction:** Coil route, do not kill him. Wait until he is on the south leg walking west. Stand on the gangway or the deck.
- **Smallest correction:** Park this for Anthony. Do not rewrite the scavenger, his sight, or his patrol. If he wants the reveal readable in peace, move the deck and gangway until the standing spot is outside that cone. If he wants the tension, change the spec's "Possible danger: none" and say so on the playtest checklist. Either answer is a placement or a spec sentence, not a new system.

### G-03 — polish

- **Where:** `Source/DeadCurrent/Save/DCLandingStageMapTest.cpp`, coil section: after the turn-in it teleports to `FarAway` (−1100, −250) and waits 1.2 s. `ADCConditionalPresence::ObservedDistance` is 1500 cm. Lookout pivot is about (3100, 1280); coil placement is (960, −990). Those centers are about 31.2 m apart, so the two 15 m bubbles do not overlap, but only by about a metre on the straight line.
- **Behavior:** The test proves deferral at the lookout and release from a point that is far from both pivots. It does not walk the shore. The real walk from the lookout, west around the ridge, then south, does cross a long stretch that is outside both bubbles, so Mara should already be on the deck before the player reaches the door, as long as she is not kept on screen the whole way. That geography is unprotected. Moving the stage north until the bubbles meet would leave the stage empty when the player arrives, and this test would stay green.
- **Why it matters:** Deferral is the difference between "she vanished in front of me" and "the landing changed while I was away." The candidate gets it right. The test does not lock the route that the walkthrough actually uses.
- **Reproduction:** Not a current failure. The gap shows up only if a later edit pulls the coil placement toward the lookout, or if a player backs away from Mara while keeping her on screen until they are already inside the stage's 15 m bubble (the door and the boathouse interior are inside that bubble). In that second case she correctly does not vanish, and the stage stays empty until they leave both bubbles.
- **Smallest correction:** In `LandingStage`, after the coil turn-in, teleport to a point on the real return path that is outside both 15 m bubbles (west of the ridge, still north of the door), call `Evaluate`, and assert the coil state there. Keep the existing "still at the lookout while standing next to her" check.

### G-04 — polish

- **Where:** `DCLandingStageMapTest.cpp` (power cut is set and cleared only in the default picture). `DCBoathouseMapTest.cpp` `ArtLayer` skips every `LandingStage` audio actor and comments that `Map.Boathouse.LandingStage` checks the hum. It does not. The hum is `Landing_BulbHum` in `build_landing_stage.py` (`ADCConditionalAudio`, `!wreck.power_cut`, volume 0.08).
- **Behavior:** Bulb lights use `ActiveConditions`, not presence, so a power cut is independent of the Shore Watch route. The test never leaves `wreck.power_cut` set through coil or combat, and never reads `IsAudible()` on the hum. On a power-cut load the hum can still start in `BeginPlay` (flags are not restored yet) and fade on the first tick (`FadeOutSeconds` 0.4). `ADCConditionalAudio` does not listen to `OnRestored`. Volume 0.08 makes this easy to miss. The same gap already exists for the relay and live-water hums.
- **Why it matters:** The plan's stage test asked for the lamp string off on either route. The light half of that is true by inspection and is checked in the default state. The hum half is only a comment.
- **Reproduction:** Load a save that already has `wreck.power_cut` while standing on the stage. Listen for a short hum that should already be dead. Separately, pull the leads after each Shore Watch route and confirm the bulbs and the hum die without moving Mara or the skiff.
- **Smallest correction:** In the landing map test, set `wreck.power_cut` once in the coil picture and once in the combat picture, assert the middle bulb is inactive and the hum is not audible, and assert Mara's rule id did not change. Do not refactor audio unless that assert fails on load; if it does, have `ADCConditionalAudio` `Evaluate()` from `OnRestored`, the same snap the presence rules already use.

## Passes

Checked in source, tests, and the logs above, and found right. Do not rework these in WS-09.

- **The state table is the flags, in the right order.** Every landing rule lists `shore.path_cleared` before `shore.relay_recovered`. Kill, then hand over the coil, stays the combat picture (tested by setting the second flag after `done_killed`). Killed but not yet told stays the default, because `shore.path_cleared` is set only on `done_killed` OnEnter (tested). Taking the coil does not change the stage until `done_coil`. No new flag, quest stage, item, or `SaveVersion`.
- **Coil, then killing him later, stays the coil picture.** `done_coil` has no transition, so `shore.path_cleared` is never set. She has a line for it (`went_back`). The landing does not unpack. That matches the plan (a drifted skiff does not come back; the reverse was not given a new flag). Not a defect.
- **`ADCConditionalPresence` is generic.** No Mara branch. Targets, ordered `FDCGameplayCondition` states, first match, hide with collision off, move with authored offsets, snap on BeginPlay and on `OnRestored`, defer otherwise. The editor test covers default, first match, offsets and yaw, hide/show collision, deferral at the current place and the destination, snap on `NotifyRestored`, replaced flags, a destroyed target, and "writes nothing / no persistent id." A second POI can author rules without editing this class.
- **It saves nothing, and load order is right.** `ReplaceFlags` does not broadcast. `ApplyPendingLoad` restores flags, world actors, and the player, then `NotifyRestored`, which snaps. Quest `ReplaceFromSaved` does not re-run OnEnter. Mara's persistent state does not store a transform, so a save cannot fight the rule. A version-5 save with no landing ids loads the picture its flags imply, tackle box full, stage undiscovered (`LandingStageSaves`). Actors absent from an older save keep authored state. Discovery polls; it does not overlap, so the load teleport does not re-banner a place already in the save (tested on the coil F9).
- **Hidden targets drop collision.** `SetActorEnableCollision(false)` with `SetActorHiddenInGame`. Interaction is a visibility sweep, so a hidden card or cut line is not a prompt. The map test checks that flag on every presence target, which is the right proxy. Shown again, authored collision comes back. Mara is moved, never hidden, so her id, dialogue, and registry entry stay one actor. She does not walk (`MaxWalkSpeed` 0). Her pack is a second rule on the same flags; the pivots are about a metre apart, inside one 15 m bubble, so they do not desync in practice.
- **Routes and collision footprint.** Gangway and deck are walkable blocks under NoCollision planks (step from beach 0, curb 18, gangway top 21, deck top 27, all under a 45 cm step). Piles, lean-to, ropes, pack, cargo, lid, and `LandingDress` are NoCollision. The lake-side walk (Y −560..−320), the door apron, the hull, and the patrol square are outside the new blocking volumes. Bulbs go dark from `wreck.power_cut` alone; the lantern does not. Inspect text does not explain the Current, name a faction, or say where anyone is going.
- **Production shape.** Gameplay lives in `build_landing_stage.py`. `build_boathouse.py` only calls `build_landing_stage_poi()`. Dressing is `dress_landing.py` / `LandingDress`, outside `RebuildContent.bat`. No POI type in C++. Stable ids in the spec (`shore.landing_stage`, `landing.tackle`; `landing.crate`, `landing.skiff`, `landing.note` reserved and unused) match the script. The next reactive POI needs content, conditions, placements, and tests. It does not need another presence system. Do not fold the POI script back into `build_boathouse.py`, and do not add a save field for placement.

## Answers

1. **State table.** Yes. Combat is first on every rule, so both flags stay the combat picture. Neither flag is the authored default (skiff moored, lantern light and card and line shown, lid leaning, contents shown, lashing and cargo and cut line hidden, Mara and pack at the lookout). Coil is `shore.relay_recovered` only. Killed but not told does not set `shore.path_cleared`, so the stage stays default. `wreck.power_cut` is only the bulbs and their hum.
2. **Stuck, fall, blocked.** The gangway and deck are a short step sequence onto solid blocks, and the props that look like obstacles (lid, ropes, pack, dressing) do not collide. Nothing new blocks the lake-side walk, the review route, the door apron, or the patrol square. Not played, so a snag on the invisible bulb volume at the east edge (a thin inspect box, same pattern as the hull) is unconfirmed and not filed.
3. **Inspectables.** Crate, lantern, and skiff variants match the three Shore Watch states, combat first. The card is a presence target and loses collision when hidden, so the combat trace cannot use it; the cold lantern says the card is gone. The cut line is hidden with collision off except in combat, where it is the cleat inspect; the adrift skiff is the same actor, moved out of the 250 cm trace, which matches "skiff or cut line." Bulbs are an invisible collision volume over the glow cubes (mesh hidden, collision on), which is how this map already does inspect hits. The test calls `Interact` directly, so it does not prove the trace; the collision flags do.
4. **Save/load.** Coil and combat each F5 on the gangway, diverge, and F9, and the following assert runs before a later rule tick, so the restore snap is what puts the picture back. Coil also checks no second banner and the tackle stack. `LandingStageSaves` is a synthetic version-5 save, which is the right "ids added later" case. `LandingStagePlayerSave` loads the real `DeadCurrent.sav` when one exists. What they miss is G-03 (the walk, not the teleport) and G-04 (power cut crossed with a route, and the hum).
5. **Mara.** She cannot be duplicated. She does not move while the player is within 15 m of her or of the deck, and the test holds that at the lookout. A normal walk west around the ridge crosses an unobserved stretch, so she should be on the deck before the door. Dialogue is by quest stage, not by where she stands; `done_coil` from the deck is tested. The scavenger does not react to her. He can react to the player standing there (G-02). Catching her mid-move would mean keeping her on screen until you are already inside the deck's 15 m bubble; then the stage stays empty until you leave. That is the deferral rule, not a teleport glitch.
6. **New saved state.** Location `shore.landing_stage` and container `landing.tackle` only. Presence rules have no persistent id and write no flag. No `SaveVersion` bump.
7. **Inspect text.** No. The card, the crate, the skiff, the lantern, the bulbs, and the cut line stay inside the spec's provisional claims. They do not explain the Current or decide who she is or where she is going. The contradiction is the spoken `reward_coil` line (G-01), not the inspect text.
8. **What Anthony will notice that the tests will not.** The reward line versus the packed boat (G-01), and, on the coil route, the scavenger starting a chase from the south leg while he is trying to look at the stage (G-02). After that, the empty lookout's crate still says her notebook lies open. That line is older than the move and can read as left behind; it was not filed. A power-cut reload may emit a very quiet hum for under half a second (G-04).
