# DEAD CURRENT - World Bible

Canon for setting, factions, characters and places. See `LongTermPlan.txt` sections 2 and 3 for the premise.

## Setting

A post-collapse Great Lakes archipelago, roughly sixty years after a cascading infrastructure disaster ended long-distance power, shipping, communications and centralized government.

## The Current

Violent electromagnetic storms, unusual electrical activity, disrupted machinery, altered wildlife behavior, and intermittent transmissions from infrastructure that should have been dead for decades. Some transmissions appear to contain voices.

## Factions

To be defined. Three broad positions exist:

- restore the buried network to rebuild regional civilization and trade
- the transmissions are evidence something new has emerged inside the network
- restoring the network gives whoever controls it unacceptable power (controls surviving industrial infrastructure)

## Institutions (provisional)

- **Great Lakes Maritime Authority**: the pre-collapse body that ran shipping, storm protocol and shoreline infrastructure. Its notices, supply crates and radio relays are everywhere on the shore. Placeholder name from the first playable; confirm or replace.
- **Maritime Authority relays**: shoreline radio relay housings, dead for sixty years. Some have started transmitting again when powered, faintly, in something close to words. Whether the words are real is deliberately unresolved.

## Locations

### First playable / Micro RPG

- Abandoned boathouse on the shoreline (player wakes here)
- Exterior shoreline path (scavenger's patrol loop)
- Scavenger camp on the path: a relay wired to a truck battery
- Mara's lookout behind the ridge, out of the scavenger's sight

## Characters

- **Mara** (provisional): watches the stretch of shore by the boathouse for "people who still listen before they shoot". Cautious, dry, practical. She has heard the relay's words herself and does not want them repeated "on the boats". If given the relay coil, she keeps it and transcribes what it says. Her role beyond Shore Watch is undecided.
- **The scavenger**: unnamed. Works a loop on the shore path and tends the relay at his camp. Why he rigged it is not established.
